Construction spec for the karigar

  • Frame: kiln-dried kikar or sheesham, glued + screwed joints, corner blocks; legs dark-walnut polish, 8 cm tall, tapered.
  • Seat: elastic webbing (or no-sag springs) + 10 cm high-density foam (40 density) + 2.5 cm soft topper. Moderately firm — should not sink more than ~3 cm.
  • Back: 7.5 cm medium foam with built-in lumbar roll; fixed, no mechanism.
  • Ottoman: plywood base box, 10 cm HD foam top; rubber pads on all four feet; the two rear feet slightly set back so the face sits flush with the chair's front rail. Optional: one small felt pad strip on the chair face to prevent rubbing.
  • Upholstery: beige/taupe textured woven fabric (≈7 m), double-stitched piping, zipped seat cover for cleaning.
  • Avoid: deep seats, soft "sofa" arms, swivel/rocker bases, glides that slide.
